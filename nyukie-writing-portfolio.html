Sample work

A quick example of how I improve writing.

Before: I want to thank you because you always there when I need someone and you make me feel that I am not alone.
After: I want to thank you for always being there when I need someone. You have a way of making me feel like I’m never alone.
Before: Our product is very good and you should buy it because it is affordable and useful.
After: Affordable, practical, and made for everyday use — a simple choice that gives you great value.
